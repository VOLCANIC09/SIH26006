Bulk Carrier Classes

Handysize

DWT: 10,000–40,000 tons
LOA: up to ~190 m
Draft: ~9–10 m
Smaller ports, flexible cargo (grain, minor bulks)

Handymax / Supramax

DWT: 40,000–65,000 tons
LOA: ~180–200 m
Often has onboard cranes (geared)

Panamax

DWT: 65,000–80,000 tons
LOA: max 294.1 m
Beam: max 32.31 m
Draft: max ~12.04 m
Sized to transit the (old) Panama Canal locks

Post-Panamax

Too large for the old Panama Canal locks (pre-2016 expansion)
DWT: 80,000–120,000 tons

Neo-Panamax (New Panamax)

LOA: max 366 m
Beam: max 51.25 m
Draft: max 15.2 m
Fits the expanded Panama Canal locks (opened 2016)

Capesize

DWT: 100,000–200,000+ tons
Beam: typically >32.3 m (too wide for Panama Canal, historically had to sail around Cape Horn/Cape of Good Hope)
Carries iron ore, coal
Draft: ~17–18 m

Valemax

DWT: ~400,000 tons
LOA: ~360–362 m
Beam: ~65 m
Built for Vale (Brazilian mining company) to carry iron ore
One of the largest bulk carriers in the world
Tanker Classes

Panamax (tanker)

DWT: ~60,000–80,000 tons

Aframax

DWT: 80,000–120,000 tons
Named after the old "Average Freight Rate Assessment" (AFRA) system
Flexible size, widely used

Suezmax

DWT: 120,000–200,000 tons
Draft: max ~20.1 m
Sized to transit the Suez Canal (loaded)
Beam typically up to ~50 m

VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier)

DWT: 200,000–320,000 tons
LOA: ~330 m
Too large for Suez Canal when fully loaded

ULCC (Ultra Large Crude Carrier)

DWT: 320,000–550,000 tons
LOA: up to ~380–415 m (historically, e.g. Seawise Giant)
Largest tankers ever built; mostly phased out due to port limitations

Malaccamax

Draft: max ~20.5–21 m
Sized to transit the Strait of Malacca
Relevant for VLCCs and large bulkers/container ships
Container Ship Classes

Feeder

Capacity: up to 3,000 TEU

Panamax (container)

Capacity: ~3,000–5,000 TEU
Beam: max 32.31 m (old canal limit)

Post-Panamax

Capacity: 5,000–10,000 TEU
Beam: >32.31 m

New Panamax (Neopanamax)

Capacity: ~12,000–14,500 TEU
Beam: max 51.25 m
Fits expanded Panama Canal

Suezmax (container)

Beam limited by Suez Canal (~77.5 m width available, but draft is the real constraint)

ULCS (Ultra Large Container Ship)

Capacity: 14,500–24,000+ TEU
LOA: up to ~400 m
Beam: ~59–61 m
Current largest class (e.g., MSC, HMM, Ever Given-class ships)
